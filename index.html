About My Soundscape

I live in the Greater Toronto Area. When I think of daily life here, I think of traffic, transit, and busy train stations. I made this short sound diary to show how a normal trip can have different sounds.

The 80-second soundscape is an original, computer-made interpretation of those sounds, not an actual field recording. The images are simple digital illustrations made for this project.
